    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Norfolk, VA

    How NAD+ Works in Norfolk

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. In every cell it carries electrons through the reactions that turn food into ATP, and it is consumed by sirtuins and PARPs, enzymes tied to DNA repair and stress responses. Older tissue tends to have less. A subcutaneous injection places NAD+ under the skin, where it is absorbed into the bloodstream without passing through the digestive tract, though how much reaches each tissue is still being studied.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Typical patients want steadier energy, clearer thinking or better recovery. People on semaglutide or tirzepatide sometimes add it. It is not a weight loss medication and does not treat any specific disease, and people who are pregnant, breastfeeding, have active cancer or significant liver or kidney disease are generally not candidates.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Norfolk

    Small shots, slow push. A common start is roughly 50 milligrams under the skin a few times weekly, with increases every week or two depending on how you feel. Belly and thigh are the usual sites. A slow injection makes a real difference.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. A wave of warmth, some queasiness or brief chest tightness right after the shot is common, and it typically passes in a few minutes. Over weeks two to four, some patients report steadier energy or clearer thinking, while others notice little change. Your provider adjusts the ramp at check-ins.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Norfolk

    Flushing leads the list. Injection site redness, stinging and soreness, along with nausea, lightheadedness and headache, are also common. Too much too quickly can cause chest pressure, cramping or palpitations, which typically ease with a smaller dose and a slower push. Hives or trouble breathing mean call 911.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. Expect questions on cancer, pregnancy, organ health, heart rhythm and whether you use NMN, NR or high-dose niacin. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Norfolk

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. IV NAD+ puts more in at once, though sessions often last hours and the discomfort during the drip is a common complaint. The nasal spray is needle-free. Oral precursors like NMN and nicotinamide riboside are supplements that help the body make its own NAD+.
