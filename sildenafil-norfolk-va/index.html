    How Sildenafil Works in Norfolk

    Sildenafil blocks PDE5. That enzyme normally breaks down cGMP, the molecule that relaxes smooth muscle in the blood vessels of the penis, so blocking it lets blood flow in more easily during arousal. Arousal is still required. The pill does not create desire or an erection out of nowhere. What it does is make an erection easier to reach and to hold.

    Who Sildenafil Is For

    The usual candidate is an adult man with erectile dysfunction who has sex now and then and would rather take a pill only when needed. Causes can be physical, psychological or both. Men with diabetes or high blood pressure often respond well, though those conditions need their own care too. Some men end up better suited to tadalafil. That is usually men who want a longer window.

    Sildenafil Dosing and Timing in Norfolk

    Take it by mouth 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Tablets come in 25, 50 and 100 mg, with 50 mg as the usual starting point. No more than one dose in any 24 hour period. A greasy dinner can push the onset back by an hour, so a lighter meal beforehand helps. The effect usually lasts four to five hours.

    What the First Few Weeks Look Like

    Plenty of men notice a difference with the first dose. Others need several attempts before the timing clicks. A fair trial is four to eight separate attempts at a given dose, because first-time nerves and a couple of drinks can blunt the effect early on. Mild headache and flushing are common early. Keep a quick note of dose, timing and results. That record is what your provider uses to adjust.

    Sildenafil Side Effects in Norfolk

    Headache, facial flushing, a stuffy nose and indigestion are the most common side effects. A temporary blue tinge or light sensitivity in vision happens to some men, usually at 100 mg. Dizziness is more likely with alcohol, with blood pressure medication and in men who stand up quickly after taking a 100 mg dose. An erection longer than four hours, or any sudden loss of vision or hearing, is an emergency room situation, not a message to your provider. Rare, but worth knowing.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule sildenafil out. That includes nitroglycerin tablets or spray, isosorbide and recreational poppers, because the combination can drop blood pressure to dangerous levels. Riociguat is also off the table. Your provider asks about heart attack or stroke in the past six months, unstable angina and severe liver or kidney disease. One more question matters: can you climb two flights of stairs without chest pain?

    Sildenafil Compared to Other ED Options in Norfolk

    Sildenafil kicks in within an hour and fades in about four to five hours. Tadalafil lasts up to 36 hours and also comes in a daily dose. Chewables, troches and rapid dissolve tablets are compounded formats, and compounded products are not FDA-approved. Because the generic is FDA-approved and identical in active ingredient to Viagra, providers often start here when a man wants a short, predictable window. Short window, long track record.
